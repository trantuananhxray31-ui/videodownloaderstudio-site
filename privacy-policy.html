Privacy Policy

Video Downloader Studio respects your privacy.

Information we use

The application uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the TikTok account chosen by the user. It may access basic account information and account statistics only to identify the connected account and display its status.

Video uploads

When the user chooses to upload a video, the application uses TikTok Content Posting API to upload the video to the user's own TikTok account as a draft. The user remains responsible for reviewing and publishing the content.

Sharing

Video Downloader Studio does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising. Users may disconnect their TikTok account at any time.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the application owner through the GitHub account associated with this website.
